Workout Schedule — Account & Data Deletion

Developer: chgus8453 · Contact: chgus8453@gmail.com

Google Play app users

The Google Play app has no accounts and stores records only on your phone; nothing is stored on a server. To delete your data, go to phone Settings → Apps → Workout Schedule → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Website / directly installed app account holders

  1. Log in at gkdl.shop/workout or in the app.
  2. Open ⚙️ Settings → 🔒 Privacy → 🗑️ Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap the delete button twice. Deletion is immediate.

Data deleted: username, nickname, email address, hashed password, and all workout plans, records and settings stored on the server. Nothing is retained, and deletion cannot be undone.

Can't log in? Email chgus8453@gmail.com from your registered address with your username. We will verify and delete your account within 7 days and let you know.
